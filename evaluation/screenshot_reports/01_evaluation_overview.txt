========================================================================
            ATTACK CORRELATION ENGINE - EVALUATION OVERVIEW
========================================================================

What was evaluated and how the results should be interpreted

------------------------------------------------------------------------
EVALUATION DATA
  Input events:             472
  Ground Truth events:      472
  Attack events:            56
  Engine stories:           183
  Engine campaigns:         105

EVALUATION DESIGN
  The engine processes the telemetry and produces stories,
  relationships, and campaigns.
  Ground Truth is used by the separate evaluator as the
  reference for measuring the engine output.

WHAT IS BEING MEASURED?
  Relationship detection: whether event links match the
  expected relationships in Ground Truth.
  Campaign reconstruction: whether attack activity is
  grouped together without excessive unrelated activity.
  Stage reconstruction: whether attack events are assigned
  to the expected attack stages.

SCOPE LIMITATION
  This evaluation contains one labelled attack scenario.
  Results demonstrate performance on this dataset; they do
  not establish generalization to all malware or attack types.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
